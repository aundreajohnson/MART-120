My Favorite Story: Cinderella

Cinderella is treated poorly by her stepmother and stepsisters, but with the help of her fairy godmother, she attends the royal ball in a magical gown and glass slippers. She dances with the prince but must leave before midnight when the magic ends. The prince finds her lost slipper, searches the kingdom, and finally discovers Cinderella. They live happily ever after.
